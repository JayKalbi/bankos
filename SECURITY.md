| Version | Supported          |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Security is our absolute priority (Zero Trust Architecture). If you discover a vulnerability, please DO NOT open a public issue. Email security@bankos.example.com. We operate on a 48-hour SLA for critical vulnerability acknowledgment.
